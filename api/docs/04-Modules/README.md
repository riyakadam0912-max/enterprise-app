# Module overview

Purpose

This page provides a concise map of the product’s major functional areas.

Who should read this

This document is for business owners, administrators, developers, and support teams who need a quick understanding of what each module is responsible for.

Overview

The product is organized into functional modules rather than a single monolithic screen. Each module handles a business area and shares common identity and reporting infrastructure.

## Main modules

### HR and people

Handles employee records, leave, attendance, payroll, and people-related operations.

### CRM and sales

Handles leads, contacts, campaigns, deals, quotes, and sales activity.

### Finance

Handles invoices, payments, expenses, ledger entries, and financial records.

### Projects and tasks

Handles project planning, assignment, status updates, reviews, and task tracking.

### Operations and support

Handles tickets, events, dynamic forms, and business process support.

### Notifications

Handles user-facing alerts, workflow signals, and delivery records.

### Reporting and analytics

Provides summary metrics and operational visibility across the platform.

## Relationship between modules

Most modules share the same identity and access system. This means users are authenticated once, then granted permission based on their role and the organization context they operate in.
